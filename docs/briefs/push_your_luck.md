# Design brief: Push-your-luck

_Repeated continue/stop decision with escalating risk._

## Why this is white space

- 24 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$4,578,081**, vs $1,782,196 for comparable games without it: a **2.4× revenue multiple**.
- 83% of them grossed $1M+, vs 65% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $610,145,325.

## Proven by

- **The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth** (2014, Action Roguelite): ~$210,277,209
- **Risk of Rain 2** (2020, Shooter Roguelite): ~$161,264,012
- **Darkest Dungeon** (2016, Strategy Roguelite): ~$123,453,030
- **FTL: Faster Than Light** (2012, Strategy Roguelite): ~$23,880,855
- **Descenders** (2019, Action Roguelite): ~$13,384,944
- **Returnal** (2023, Shooter Roguelite): ~$11,079,223
- **Loop Hero** (2021, Auto-battler Roguelite): ~$9,963,693
- **Spelunky 2** (2020, Action Roguelite): ~$8,000,874

## Pair it with (over-represented in its top earners)

- **Daily / Weekly Challenge**: in 56% of top earners (8.4× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Gambling / Chance Rewards**: in 44% of top earners (7.6× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Companions / Pets**: in 44% of top earners (6.5× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Leaderboards**: in 56% of top earners (6.1× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Challenge-gated Unlocks**: in 56% of top earners (5.3× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Multiple Endings / True Ending**: in 56% of top earners (4.8× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Secrets / Hidden Areas**: in 44% of top earners (4.8× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Codex / Compendium**: in 44% of top earners (4.8× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Active Items**: in 44% of top earners (4.5× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Mod Support / Workshop**: in 44% of top earners (4.2× its rate across curated roguelikes)

## Where it lives

Action Roguelite (10), Strategy Roguelite (6), Shooter Roguelite (3), Puzzle Roguelite (2), Survival Roguelite (2), Auto-battler Roguelite (1), Narrative / Event Roguelite (1), Builder Roguelite (1), Roguelike Deckbuilder (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $19.99

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
