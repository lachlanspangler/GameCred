# Design brief: Mid-run Save & Quit

_A run can be suspended and resumed later (no save-scumming)._

## Why this is white space

- 38 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$5,384,910**, vs $2,402,947 for comparable games without it: a **2.4× revenue multiple**.
- 82% of them grossed $1M+, vs 75% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $1,051,260,840.

## Proven by

- **The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth** (2014, Action Roguelite): ~$209,793,381
- **Hades** (2020, Action Roguelite): ~$140,569,042
- **Darkest Dungeon** (2016, Strategy Roguelite): ~$123,336,614
- **Slay the Spire** (2019, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$120,632,178
- **Dead Cells** (2018, Roguevania): ~$101,314,008
- **Slay the Spire 2** (2026, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$84,490,740
- **Balatro** (2024, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$49,450,429
- **Enter the Gungeon** (2016, Action Roguelite): ~$40,816,170

- **Codex / Compendium**: in 67% of top earners (7.3× its rate across curated roguelikes)

- **Double-edged Effects**: in 83% of top earners (5.2× its rate across curated roguelikes)

## Where it lives

Roguelike Deckbuilder (10), Traditional Roguelike (9), Strategy Roguelite (8), Action Roguelite (5), Narrative / Event Roguelite (2), Roguevania (1), Shooter Roguelite (1), Puzzle Roguelite (1), Survival Roguelite (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $24.99

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
