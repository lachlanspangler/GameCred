# Design brief: Multiplicative Scaling

_Multipliers compound (xMult, % stacking) enabling exponential builds._

## Why this is white space

- 29 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$5,852,922**, vs $1,984,947 for comparable games without it: a **2.4× revenue multiple**.
- 76% of them grossed $1M+, vs 68% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $711,508,404.

## Proven by

- **The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth** (2014, Action Roguelite): ~$209,793,381
- **Risk of Rain 2** (2020, Shooter Roguelite): ~$160,915,218
- **Slay the Spire** (2019, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$120,632,178
- **Balatro** (2024, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$49,450,429
- **Noita** (2020, Action Roguelite): ~$32,396,064
- **Vampire Survivors** (2022, Survivors-like): ~$21,933,138
- **Megabonk** (2025, Survivors-like): ~$17,641,301
- **Crypt of the NecroDancer** (2015, Action Roguelite): ~$12,039,485

## Where it lives

Action Roguelite (13), Puzzle Roguelite (6), Roguelike Deckbuilder (5), Survivors-like (4), Traditional Roguelike (4), Shooter Roguelite (3), Strategy Roguelite (2), Auto-battler Roguelite (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $14.99

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
