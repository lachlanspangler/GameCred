# Design brief: Pre-run Mutators / Challenge Runs

_Selectable rule-changing modifiers or named challenge runs before starting (Isaac challenges, Noita modes)._

## Why this is white space

- 18 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$14,549,059**, vs $3,257,121 for comparable games without it: a **4.3× revenue multiple**.
- 83% of them grossed $1M+, vs 81% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $802,768,229.

## Proven by

- **The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth** (2014, Action Roguelite): ~$209,793,381
- **Risk of Rain 2** (2020, Shooter Roguelite): ~$160,915,218
- **Slay the Spire** (2019, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$120,632,178
- **Dead Cells** (2018, Roguevania): ~$101,314,008
- **Balatro** (2024, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$49,450,429
- **Enter the Gungeon** (2016, Action Roguelite): ~$40,816,170
- **Noita** (2020, Action Roguelite): ~$32,396,064
- **Vampire Survivors** (2022, Survivors-like): ~$21,933,138

- **Gambling / Chance Rewards**: in 40% of top earners (7.0× its rate across curated roguelikes)

## Where it lives

Action Roguelite (12), Roguelike Deckbuilder (9), Shooter Roguelite (2), Roguevania (2), Traditional Roguelike (2), Survivors-like (1), Builder Roguelite (1), Strategy Roguelite (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $22.49

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
