# Design brief: In-run Clock / Day Cycle

_An in-run day count or clock structures exploration and forces the climax (Ravenswatch, Atomicrops)._

## Why this is white space

- 25 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$4,788,859**, vs $1,317,010 for comparable games without it: a **2.8× revenue multiple**.
- 80% of them grossed $1M+, vs 57% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $307,830,518.

## Proven by

- **ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN** (2025, Action Roguelite): ~$127,025,496
- **Don't Starve** (2013, Survival Roguelite): ~$34,345,652
- **DEATHLOOP** (2021, Shooter Roguelite): ~$33,253,606
- **Content Warning** (2024, Action Roguelite): ~$21,621,336
- **For The King** (2018, Strategy Roguelite): ~$19,316,457
- **Loop Hero** (2021, Auto-battler Roguelite): ~$9,963,693
- **Ravenswatch** (2024, Action Roguelite): ~$9,525,351
- **PlateUp!** (2022, Strategy Roguelite): ~$9,182,606

- **Revive / Extra Life**: in 43% of top earners (4.5× its rate across curated roguelikes)

- **Rarity Tiers**: in 43% of top earners (2.3× its rate across curated roguelikes)

- **Unlockable Characters**: in 50% of top earners (1.3× its rate across curated roguelikes)

## Where it lives

Strategy Roguelite (11), Action Roguelite (10), Survival Roguelite (8), Shooter Roguelite (3), Traditional Roguelike (3), Auto-battler Roguelite (2), Narrative / Event Roguelite (2), Builder Roguelite (2), Survivors-like (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $19.99

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
