# Design brief: In-run Clock / Day Cycle

_An in-run day count or clock structures exploration and forces the climax (Ravenswatch, Atomicrops)._

## Why this is white space

- 21 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$5,638,166**, vs $1,468,559 for comparable games without it: a **3.2× revenue multiple**.
- 81% of them grossed $1M+, vs 59% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $283,236,234.

## Proven by

- **ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN** (2025, Action Roguelite): ~$126,915,963
- **Don't Starve** (2013, Survival Roguelite): ~$34,334,471
- **DEATHLOOP** (2021, Shooter Roguelite): ~$33,240,540
- **For The King** (2018, Strategy Roguelite): ~$19,305,462
- **Loop Hero** (2021, Auto-battler Roguelite): ~$9,958,523
- **Ravenswatch** (2024, Action Roguelite): ~$9,483,705
- **PlateUp!** (2022, Strategy Roguelite): ~$9,128,184
- **Tribes of Midgard** (2021, Survival Roguelite): ~$8,453,308

## Where it lives

Strategy Roguelite (10), Action Roguelite (9), Survival Roguelite (8), Shooter Roguelite (3), Traditional Roguelike (3), Builder Roguelite (2), Auto-battler Roguelite (1), Narrative / Event Roguelite (1), Survivors-like (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $19.99

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
