# Design brief: Luck Stat

_An explicit luck stat skews random outcomes (Isaac, Brotato, Vampire Survivors)._

## Why this is white space

- 15 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$9,859,616**, vs $2,431,952 for comparable games without it: a **3.6× revenue multiple**.
- 93% of them grossed $1M+, vs 74% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $538,699,963.

## Proven by

- **The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth** (2014, Action Roguelite): ~$209,793,381
- **Risk of Rain 2** (2020, Shooter Roguelite): ~$160,915,218
- **Enter the Gungeon** (2016, Action Roguelite): ~$40,816,170
- **Mewgenics** (2026, Strategy Roguelite): ~$27,187,225
- **Vampire Survivors** (2022, Survivors-like): ~$21,933,138
- **For The King** (2018, Strategy Roguelite): ~$19,305,462
- **Megabonk** (2025, Survivors-like): ~$17,641,301
- **Brotato** (2023, Survivors-like): ~$9,859,616

## Where it lives

Action Roguelite (7), Strategy Roguelite (6), Survivors-like (5), Puzzle Roguelite (3), Shooter Roguelite (2), Auto-battler Roguelite (2), Traditional Roguelike (1), Roguelike Deckbuilder (1), Builder Roguelite (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $14.99

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
