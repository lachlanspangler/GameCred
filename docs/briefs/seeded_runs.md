# Design brief: Seeded Runs

_Players can enter/share a seed to replay an identical run._

## Why this is white space

- 16 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$8,561,807**, vs $3,510,106 for comparable games without it: a **2.4× revenue multiple**.
- 75% of them grossed $1M+, vs 81% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $565,636,926.

## Proven by

- **The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth** (2014, Action Roguelite): ~$209,793,381
- **Slay the Spire** (2019, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$120,632,178
- **Dead Cells** (2018, Roguevania): ~$101,314,008
- **Balatro** (2024, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$49,450,429
- **Noita** (2020, Action Roguelite): ~$32,396,064
- **Descenders** (2019, Action Roguelite): ~$13,357,455
- **Crypt of the NecroDancer** (2015, Action Roguelite): ~$12,039,485
- **PlateUp!** (2022, Strategy Roguelite): ~$9,128,184

## Pair it with (over-represented in its top earners)

- **Pre-run Mutators / Challenge Runs**: in 62% of top earners (13.5× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Daily / Weekly Challenge**: in 75% of top earners (11.4× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Multiplicative Scaling**: in 62% of top earners (8.0× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Mod Support / Workshop**: in 75% of top earners (7.1× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Mid-run Save & Quit**: in 75% of top earners (7.0× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Leaderboards**: in 62% of top earners (6.9× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Reroll**: in 62% of top earners (6.7× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Codex / Compendium**: in 62% of top earners (6.7× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Optional Challenge Rooms**: in 88% of top earners (4.7× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Double-edged Effects**: in 75% of top earners (4.6× its rate across curated roguelikes)

## Where it lives

Action Roguelite (13), Roguelike Deckbuilder (3), Puzzle Roguelite (2), Traditional Roguelike (2), Roguevania (1), Strategy Roguelite (1), Survival Roguelite (1), Survivors-like (1), Shooter Roguelite (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $19.99

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
