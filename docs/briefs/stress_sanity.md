# Design brief: Stress / Sanity Meter

_A secondary meter (stress, sanity, corruption) that causes afflictions as it fills (Darkest Dungeon)._

## Why this is white space

- 16 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$3,297,020**, vs $1,149,943 for comparable games without it: a **3.1× revenue multiple**.
- 88% of them grossed $1M+, vs 54% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $234,728,888.

## Proven by

- **Darkest Dungeon** (2016, Strategy Roguelite): ~$123,336,614
- **Don't Starve** (2013, Survival Roguelite): ~$34,334,471
- **Darkest Dungeon II** (2023, Strategy Roguelite): ~$17,929,037
- **Stoneshard** (2020, Traditional Roguelike): ~$16,263,152
- **Dwarf Fortress** (2022, Builder Roguelite): ~$15,627,384
- **Cultist Simulator** (2018, Narrative / Event Roguelite): ~$6,291,053
- **Sunless Sea** (2015, Narrative / Event Roguelite): ~$5,502,058
- **Pixel Piracy** (2014, Strategy Roguelite): ~$3,336,920

## Pair it with (over-represented in its top earners)

- **Random Events (? nodes)**: in 47% of top earners (1.6× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Turn-based**: in 47% of top earners (1.4× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Distinct Characters / Classes**: in 50% of top earners (1.0× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Real-time Action**: in 44% of top earners (0.7× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Permadeath**: in 59% of top earners (0.6× its rate across curated roguelikes)

## Where it lives

Action Roguelite (38), Strategy Roguelite (16), Survival Roguelite (10), Shooter Roguelite (7), Traditional Roguelike (5), Narrative / Event Roguelite (5), Roguelike Deckbuilder (4), Survivors-like (4), Puzzle Roguelite (4), Roguevania (2), Builder Roguelite (1), Auto-battler Roguelite (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $19.49

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
