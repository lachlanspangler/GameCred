# Design brief: Multi-phase Bosses

_Bosses change behaviour across phases._

## Why this is white space

- 19 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$11,585,045**, vs $2,334,902 for comparable games without it: a **3.3× revenue multiple**.
- 89% of them grossed $1M+, vs 74% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $585,797,959.

## Proven by

- **Hades** (2020, Action Roguelite): ~$140,569,042
- **ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN** (2025, Action Roguelite): ~$126,915,963
- **Dead Cells** (2018, Roguevania): ~$101,314,008
- **Hades II** (2025, Action Roguelite): ~$61,459,002
- **Enter the Gungeon** (2016, Action Roguelite): ~$40,816,170
- **FTL: Faster Than Light** (2012, Strategy Roguelite): ~$23,869,070
- **Skul: The Hero Slayer** (2021, Action Roguelite): ~$20,937,893
- **BlazBlue Entropy Effect** (2024, Action Roguelite): ~$12,172,891

- **Ascension / Heat Ladder**: in 71% of top earners (6.4× its rate across curated roguelikes)

## Where it lives

Action Roguelite (12), Roguevania (3), Strategy Roguelite (2), Roguelike Deckbuilder (2), Shooter Roguelite (1), Traditional Roguelike (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $24.99

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
