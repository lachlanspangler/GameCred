# Design brief: Companions / Pets

_Autonomous companions or familiars that follow and assist the player (Isaac familiars, Neon Abyss pets)._

## Why this is white space

- 26 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$6,704,075**, vs $2,302,467 for comparable games without it: a **2.4× revenue multiple**.
- 85% of them grossed $1M+, vs 73% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $610,906,304.

## Proven by

- **The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth** (2014, Action Roguelite): ~$209,793,381
- **Risk of Rain 2** (2020, Shooter Roguelite): ~$160,915,218
- **Hades II** (2025, Action Roguelite): ~$61,459,002
- **Enter the Gungeon** (2016, Action Roguelite): ~$40,816,170
- **Don't Starve** (2013, Survival Roguelite): ~$34,334,471
- **Barony** (2015, Traditional Roguelike): ~$11,072,616
- **Moonlighter** (2018, Action Roguelite): ~$10,893,351
- **The Binding of Isaac** (2011, Action Roguelite): ~$9,511,957

## Pair it with (over-represented in its top earners)

- **Luck Stat**: in 50% of top earners (12.8× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Cursed Items**: in 75% of top earners (7.6× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Secrets / Hidden Areas**: in 62% of top earners (7.3× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Multiple Endings / True Ending**: in 62% of top earners (5.9× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Codex / Compendium**: in 50% of top earners (5.5× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Revive / Extra Life**: in 50% of top earners (5.3× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Active Items**: in 50% of top earners (5.2× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Mod Support / Workshop**: in 50% of top earners (5.1× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **High-variance Loot**: in 50% of top earners (4.9× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Elites / Champions**: in 62% of top earners (4.2× its rate across curated roguelikes)

## Where it lives

Action Roguelite (14), Traditional Roguelike (6), Survival Roguelite (2), Roguelike Deckbuilder (2), Shooter Roguelite (1), Survivors-like (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $17.49

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
