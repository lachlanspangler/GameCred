# Design brief: Codex / Compendium

_Collectible encyclopedia of discovered items/enemies._

## Why this is white space

- 35 paid roguelikes use it (low competition). Their median gross is **$6,253,060**, vs $2,089,137 for comparable games without it: a **2.6× revenue multiple**.
- 80% of them grossed $1M+, vs 69% of comparable games.
- Total market for games using it is $1,056,531,290.

## Proven by

- **The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth** (2014, Action Roguelite): ~$209,793,381
- **Risk of Rain 2** (2020, Shooter Roguelite): ~$160,915,218
- **Hades** (2020, Action Roguelite): ~$140,569,042
- **Slay the Spire** (2019, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$120,632,178
- **Dead Cells** (2018, Roguevania): ~$101,314,008
- **Hades II** (2025, Action Roguelite): ~$61,459,002
- **Balatro** (2024, Roguelike Deckbuilder): ~$49,450,429
- **Enter the Gungeon** (2016, Action Roguelite): ~$40,816,170

## Pair it with (over-represented in its top earners)

- **Pre-run Mutators / Challenge Runs**: in 60% of top earners (12.8× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Secrets / Hidden Areas**: in 53% of top earners (6.2× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Multiplicative Scaling**: in 47% of top earners (6.2× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Mid-run Save & Quit**: in 60% of top earners (6.1× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Multiple Endings / True Ending**: in 60% of top earners (5.6× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Reroll**: in 53% of top earners (5.5× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Revive / Extra Life**: in 47% of top earners (5.0× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Controller / Deck Friendly**: in 47% of top earners (4.8× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **Active Items**: in 47% of top earners (4.8× its rate across curated roguelikes)
- **High-variance Loot**: in 47% of top earners (4.6× its rate across curated roguelikes)

## Where it lives

Action Roguelite (19), Strategy Roguelite (6), Shooter Roguelite (5), Survivors-like (5), Roguelike Deckbuilder (3), Traditional Roguelike (3), Roguevania (2), Narrative / Event Roguelite (2), Puzzle Roguelite (1)

Typical launch price of the top earners: $24.99

## Caveats

Small-n association: a few breakout hits can drive the multiple. Treat this as a lead to test, not a guarantee. Regenerated on every export.
