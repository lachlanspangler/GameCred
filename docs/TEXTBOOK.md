## Run Structure

_How a single run is shaped, bounded and repeated._

### Death & Persistence

**Permadeath** (`permadeath`) — Losing ends the run; the run's state is discarded.
  
  Δ -6.0 (95% CI -14.9…+2.3) · **neutral** · in 28% of games · tagging κ -0.07 · declining -1.7 pp/yr
  
  Core in: The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth, Risk of Rain 2, Slay the Spire, Dead Cells, Don't Starve · also: Gunfire Reborn, Enter the Gungeon, Noita, FTL: Faster Than Light
  
  > *Realm of the Mad God Exalt*: Character death is permanent and destroys all equipped gear; only vault storage survives.

**Mid-run Save & Quit** (`mid_run_save`) — A run can be suspended and resumed later (no save-scumming).
  
  Δ +5.7 (95% CI -0.3…+11.3) · **risk reducer** · in 3% of games · tagging κ 0.78 · declining -0.5 pp/yr
  
  Core in: — · also: PlateUp!, Bad North: Jotunn Edition, Across the Obelisk, Caves of Qud

**Revive / Extra Life** (`second_chance`) — Items or systems that prevent a single death from ending the run.
  
  Δ +2.7 (95% CI -4.5…+10.5) · **risk reducer** · in 2% of games
  
  Core in: For The King, For The King II · also: The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth, Hades, Vampire Survivors, ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN
  
  > *For The King*: A shared life pool lets fallen party members be revived until it runs out.

**Extraction / Bank-out** (`extraction`) — Player may leave a run early to keep loot; dying forfeits it.
  
  Δ +4.7 (95% CI -2.7…+12.1) · **risk reducer** · in 1% of games · tagging κ 0.65
  
  Core in: Loop Hero, Moonlighter, Witchfire, Quasimorph, Turnip Boy Robs a Bank · also: Survivor Mercs, ZERO Sievert, Realm of the Mad God Exalt, SULFUR
  
  > *Loop Hero*: Retreating at camp keeps all gathered resources; dying keeps only 30%.

**Heirs / Legacy Inheritance** (`legacy_inheritance`) — The next run's character is an heir with rolled traits or inherits from the fallen (Rogue Legacy).
  
  effect: insufficient data · in 1% of games
  
  Core in: Rogue Legacy, Rogue Legacy 2 · also: Wildermyth, Sunless Sea, The Last Stand: Aftermath, Heat Signature
  
  > *Rogue Legacy 2*: Each run picks one of several heirs with random classes and traits.

**Breeding / Genetics** (`breeding_genetics`) — New units are bred from previous ones, passing traits (Mewgenics).
  
  effect: insufficient data · in 0% of games · tagging κ 0.00
  
  Core in: Mewgenics · also: Genesis Alpha One
  
  > *Mewgenics*: Cats bred in the house pass stats, abilities and mutations to kittens.
